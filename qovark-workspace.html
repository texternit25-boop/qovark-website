How confidential review works

Encrypted before upload: your file and instructions are encrypted on this device. The relay stores encrypted content and a random case reference.

Private reviewer assignment: QOVARK assigns an eligible active reviewer whose approved categories match your selection. No reviewer directory or identity is exposed to you.

Controlled access: the reviewer must authenticate with Firebase, receive a one-time coordinator code and unlock their locally-held private key.

Important limits: a reviewer can read and copy a document after decrypting it. Remove secrets you do not want the reviewer to see. Hosting providers may process technical metadata such as IP address and request time.

VOICE CONTROLS

Voice Assistant Guide

Speak to QOVARK or listen to an answer.

Use voice input

When the message field is empty, tap the microphone control. Speak clearly; the recognised words appear in the message field and are submitted when recognition finishes.

Automatic read-aloud

Tap the speaker control once to turn automatic read-aloud on. New AI replies will be spoken as they arrive. Tap again to turn it off; hold the control to open voice settings.

Adjust the voice

Press and hold the speaker control for about four seconds to open Voice Settings. Choose an installed voice and adjust speed or pitch.

Voice playback requires a Text-to-Speech voice installed on your phone. Voice input requires microphone permission and a supported speech-recognition service.

FAST CHAT

Chat Guide

Write useful prompts and work with responses.

01
Write a clear prompt

Enter your question or instructions in the message field. On a phone, use the keyboard's Enter key to add a new line; tap the send button when ready.

02
Use response actions

Use the actions below a reply to copy it, provide feedback or request another response when those controls are available.

03
Read wide tables

When an answer contains a wide table, swipe horizontally inside the table area. The message bubble stays within the chat width.

For better results, state what should be checked, the audience and whether you prefer a summary, step-by-step explanation or structured report.
