04 / PRIVACY & SECURITY

Clarity about what
is protected — and how.

QOVARK treats specialist review as a separate workflow with specific encryption steps. We believe trust requires explaining both the safeguards and their limits.

Understand the security model ↗
01

Encrypted before the review relay

Expert-review files and instructions are encrypted on the user's device before they are relayed.

02

Controlled reviewer workflow

Eligible reviewers are assigned through service categories; there is no public reviewer directory.

!

Encryption has limits

A reviewer can read and copy material after decrypting it. Share only what is necessary, and review the full security explanation before submitting sensitive content.

The security details above describe the Expert Review workflow, not a claim that every QOVARK feature uses the same encryption model.
