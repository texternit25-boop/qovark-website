Confidential review workflow

The app encrypts review material on your device before the relay receives it. Reviewers are assigned according to active status and approved categories.

Important privacy information

Understand what encryption does—and does not do.

Before upload

Your review file and instructions are encrypted on your device. The relay stores encrypted content and a random case reference.

Reviewer assignment

QOVARK assigns an eligible active reviewer whose approved categories match the request. The app does not display a public reviewer directory.

Controlled access

The reviewer must authenticate, receive a one-time coordinator code and unlock a locally held private key before opening the material.

What remains possible

Once decrypted, a reviewer can read and copy the document. Remove passwords, access tokens and details the reviewer does not need. Hosting providers may process technical metadata such as IP address and request time.

Reviewer availability and category eligibility are checked inside the workspace before a request is submitted.
