How do I sign in to QOVARK?

Open the workspace and choose email-and-password sign-in or Continue with Google. QOVARK also has a separate one-time email-code verification step for the account.

What is the difference between Fast Chat and Deep Analysis?

Fast Chat is for everyday questions, drafting and conversation. Deep Analysis is a dedicated workspace for supported files, task categories, detailed instructions and structured reports.

Where do my files and reports appear?

Files organizes uploaded resources and folders. Pages collects generated Deep Analysis reports and completed service work. Alerts shows completion updates and processing information.

Does Expert Review expose my document to a human?

Yes. The workflow encrypts material on your device before it is relayed, but an assigned reviewer can read and copy the document after decrypting it. Share only the material the reviewer needs.

Does biometric unlock replace account sign-in?

No. Fingerprint or face authentication is an optional Android app-reopening control when supported. It does not replace Firebase account authentication, QOVARK email verification or server-side access checks.

Are voice features available on every device?

Voice input depends on microphone permission and a supported speech-recognition service. Read-aloud depends on an installed text-to-speech voice. Availability varies by device and browser.

What are the upload limits and processing times?

Limits, accepted formats, usage eligibility and processing estimates can vary. The live workspace displays the requirements and information applicable to your account and task.
