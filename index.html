Three connected ways to move work forward.

QOVARK is not limited to document processing. Use the part of the workspace that fits the task, then move into a deeper workflow when the work calls for it.

01

Fast Chat

A place to ask questions, explore ideas, understand topics, draft content and work through everyday tasks in conversation.

  • Start with a question or an idea.
  • Refine the result through follow-up prompts.
  • Review important information before relying on it.
02

Deep Analysis

A more structured workflow for tasks that need additional context, supporting files or a longer set of instructions. Documents are one possible input—not the whole purpose.

  • Bring task instructions and relevant context together.
  • Work through more involved questions and analysis.
  • Availability and usage limits may depend on your account and current service configuration.
03

Expert Review

A separate route for eligible work that may benefit from feedback by a specialist, rather than relying only on an AI-generated response.

  • Submit work when the review service is available and the case is eligible.
  • Specialist feedback is distinct from AI assistance.
  • Reviewer availability and review terms can vary.

Specific features, file limits, providers and service availability can change. The signed-in QOVARK workspace is the source for your current account access and limits.

Common questions

A few things worth knowing.

Straightforward answers about what the workspace is for and how to use it thoughtfully.

Is QOVARK only for documents?

No. QOVARK is a broader AI workspace for conversation, idea development and more involved tasks. Files and documents can add context to Deep Analysis, but they are not the only reason to use the platform.

What is the difference between Fast Chat and Deep Analysis?

Fast Chat is intended for everyday conversational tasks. Deep Analysis is intended for work that benefits from more context, supporting material or a structured, longer workflow. Access and usage limits may differ.

Does Expert Review mean an AI answer is checked by a person?

Expert Review is a separate workflow for eligible submissions where specialist feedback may be available. It should not be assumed that every Fast Chat or Deep Analysis response is reviewed by a person.

Are all QOVARK features protected in exactly the same way?

Do not assume that every feature has identical data handling. The encrypted-material and device-side verification statements on this page refer to the expert-review workflow. Check the current in-product privacy information for the feature you are using.

Can I rely on an AI response as professional advice?

No. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. For important legal, medical, financial or other professional decisions, verify information with an appropriately qualified professional. A specialist review, where offered, has its own scope and terms.

Why might a feature not be available to my account?

Feature access can depend on account status, the plan or usage allowance assigned to the account, service availability and the eligibility rules for a particular workflow. Check the signed-in workspace for current access information.
