SECURITY & PRIVACY

Trust begins with
clear boundaries.

QOVARK uses distinct controls for account access, protected workspace items and confidential expert review. They serve different purposes and should not be confused with one another.

Sign in to manage security ↗
⬡

Separate layers of protection

Account authentication, QOVARK email verification, Security PIN checks and optional Android device authentication are separate controls.

Account sign-in

The app offers email-and-password sign-in and Continue with Google through Firebase Authentication. Browser Google sign-in uses a popup; Android uses its native Google sign-in bridge.

QOVARK email verification

The app has a separate QOVARK one-time email-code verification step. Firebase account sign-in alone does not necessarily mean the QOVARK verification step is complete.

Security PIN

The account Security PIN can protect selected workspace areas and protected reports. Repeated incorrect attempts can trigger a temporary lock.

Android biometrics

Fingerprint or face authentication is an optional Android app-reopening control when supported by the device and native app integration. It does not replace account sign-in or server-side authorization.

Expert Review encryption

A careful workflow with real-world limits.

Encrypted before the review relay.

Review files and instructions are encrypted on the user's device before upload to the relay. A reviewer must authenticate, receive a one-time coordinator code and unlock a locally held private key to access them.

✓Relay receives encrypted content and a random case reference.
!After decrypting, a reviewer can read and copy the document.
!Hosting providers may process technical metadata. Do not upload passwords, PINs or unrelated credentials.
